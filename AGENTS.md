# Agent instructions

## Studio interface

The studio is a photo editor, and its users know other photo editors such as Lightroom and
Apple Photos. When adding or changing an interaction or a part of the interface:

- Check how Lightroom and Apple Photos handle it. Look at other editors when those two
  differ or have no equivalent.
- Follow the established convention where one applies, and name it when describing the
  work. For example, a flag for rejected photos would take Lightroom's X key.
- When a request differs from the convention, say so before building.
- Depart from the convention where another design serves the app better, or where the
  feature has no counterpart in those editors. Say where the design departs and why.
